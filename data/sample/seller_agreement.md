# Seller Agreement

Sellers must confirm new orders within 24 hours and ship confirmed orders
within 3 business days.

Sellers must provide accurate product descriptions and images. Sellers are
responsible for defective, counterfeit, or incorrectly described products.

Seller messages must be answered within 2 business days. Sellers must accept
eligible returns according to the company return policy.

Repeated late shipments may result in temporary listing suspension. Seller
payouts are released 7 days after confirmed delivery.
