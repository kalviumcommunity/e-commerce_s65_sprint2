# Refund Policy

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.

Bank refunds usually appear within 5 to 10 business days. Card refunds usually
appear within 3 to 7 business days.

Shipping fees are refunded when the company shipped the wrong product or the
product arrived damaged.

Duplicate charges are reviewed within 5 business days. Refunds cannot be sent
to a different payment method from the original transaction.
